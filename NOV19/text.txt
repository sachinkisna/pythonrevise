
E-commerce Discount Calculator
Production Challenge
Build a shopping discount calculator using these business rules:
Purchase amount	Discount
Below ₹1,000	0%
₹1,000–₹4,999	5%
₹5,000–₹9,999	10%
₹10,000 or above	20%

Requirements:
1. Take customer name and purchase amount.
2. Reject negative purchase amounts.
3. Determine discount percentage using conditions.
4. Calculate discount amount.
5. Calculate final payable amount.
6. Display a formatted invoice using f-strings.
7. Handle invalid numeric input using try-except.
Bonus: Use a ternary expression to determine whether the customer receives a discount.
